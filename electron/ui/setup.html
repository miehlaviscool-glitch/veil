Welcome to Veil

Connect your Discord bot in under a minute. Your token is encrypted with your operating system's secure storage and is only ever sent to Discord to log your bot in.

1Connect your bot

Where do I find my token?
  1. Open the Discord Developer Portal and pick (or create) your application.
  2. Go to the Bot tab, then press Reset Token and copy it.
  3. Paste it above. You do not need to enable any privileged intents.

2Access & alerts (all optional)
